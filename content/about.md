I’m a Linux-first technologist with a longstanding interest in privacy, security, open source, and digital autonomy.
My professional life has taken me through IT support, infrastructure, networking, systems administration, and the wonderfully unpredictable intersection where technology meets actual human beings.
I like understanding how things work, figuring out why they don’t, and leaving systems—and the people using them—in a better place than I found them.


Outside of work, I’m a lifelong learner, habitual overthinker, a word nerd, and occasional rabbit-hole deep-diver.
I’m interested in technology, understanding more each day about the natural world, big ideas, writing, and the strange corners of the internet that tend to consume an unreasonable amount of time. This means YOU [YouShouldHaveSeenTHis](http://www.youshouldhaveseenthis.com/) and [TheUselessWeb](https://theuselessweb.com/).


I value depth over spectacle, meaningful connection over performance, and signal over noise.


This site is still a work in progress. So, you could say it suits me. For now, consider it the index to my various projects, experiments, discussions, diatribes, and other documented digital detours. There’s more coming once I stop reorganizing the blueprint and actually put pen to paper.
